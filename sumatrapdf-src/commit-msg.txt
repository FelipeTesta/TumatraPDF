fix(autoscroll): pixel-based ETA + TC2 2x auto + shift-hold speed halve

Fix ETA bug where it would decrease to zero by itself mid-page: replaced
page-count + wall-clock countdown with pixel-based calculation
(remainingPx = canvasSize.dy - viewPort.y) recalculated every tick.
The ETA now always reflects the true scroll position, never drifts.

TC2 double-time is automatic: 2N virtual pages = 2x canvas height =
correct 2x ETA with no special case needed.

When shift-hold is active during TC2 autoscroll (reading full screen
width), speed is temporarily halved (0.5x multiplier) to account for
slower reading of full-width text. Returns to normal on Shift release.

Removed etaStartTick and etaPageNo fields from AutoScrollState (no
longer needed with pixel-based approach).
